Who we are

The Indian River Airboat and Conservation Association brings together people who love Florida's wetlands. Many of us grew up on these waters, and we want our kids and grandkids to enjoy them the same way.

We work with landowners, agencies and other user groups to keep the marshes open, clean and safe. We also teach good airboating practices to new riders.

What we do

Conservation

Marsh cleanups, trash pickups at ramps, and support for projects that keep water levels and habitat healthy.

Safety and education

Rider safety, boating etiquette, noise courtesy near homes, and respect for wildlife and other people on the water.

Community and access

Rides, cookouts and meetings for members, and a unified voice on rules that affect access to the marsh.
